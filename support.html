Subscription

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 14 days free. You start the trial at the end of setup by choosing a monthly or yearly plan; Apple asks you to confirm with your Apple ID, but nothing is charged for the first 14 days. When the trial ends, the plan you chose begins and is billed by Apple, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in which case you are never charged. The trial is available once per Apple ID. Settings → Subscription shows how many days are left. Your training history stays exactly where it is either way.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in iOS rather than in Gamut:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Gamut, then Cancel Subscription.

You can also get there from Settings → Subscription → Manage in Gamut. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep access until the period you have already paid for runs out. Deleting the app, or your Gamut account, does not cancel the subscription.

I was charged, but the app still asks me to subscribe

Tap Restore Purchases, on the subscription screen or in Settings → Subscription. That asks the App Store to send your purchase to the app again. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with, and to the same Gamut account: a subscription unlocks only the Gamut account that bought it. If it still does not appear, email us.

Can I switch from monthly to yearly?

Yes. Choose Switch to Yearly in Settings → Subscription. The yearly plan starts straight away, your monthly plan ends, and Apple refunds the unused part of the month. You can only hold one Gamut plan at a time.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for subscriptions bought in the app are handled by Apple, not by us; we have no way to issue them. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My team covers me. Do I still need my own subscription?

No. While you are on a team with an active team plan, the team covers you, and Settings → Subscription says so. If you were already paying Apple yourself, that subscription keeps billing until you cancel it in your Apple ID settings (see "How do I cancel?"). If you leave the team later, you can subscribe again on your own and keep all your history.

Training

Why can I only generate one workout a day?

Because a training block is a sequence, not a menu. Each day is built from where you are in the block, what you did recently and how you rated it. Generating several at once would mean guessing at ratings that do not exist yet.

My first workout has no weights

Gamut won't guess at a number it has nothing to base on. A main lift with no weight shows Finding your working weight: pick a weight you could lift for the reps with a few left in the tank, log it and rate how it felt. To give Gamut your numbers up front, tap Log a Lift on Home and enter a recent set for each big lift. Logged sets replace any guess within a session or two.

The weights look wrong for a lift I've never done

For a main lift with no logged history, Gamut estimates a starting weight from conventional strength ratios and deliberately biases it low, because on an unfamiliar lift the limiter is usually technique. Log a set and rate it. If it was easy, the app steps the number up next session, and once you have real logged sets it uses those instead of the estimate.

I don't have the equipment for an exercise, or I don't want to do it

Tap the ⋯ menu on the exercise card. Swap replaces it with another exercise for the same muscles that fits your equipment, keeping the same sets and reps. Skip sets it aside for today. If you skip the same exercise in two or more recent sessions, Gamut stops programming it for the rest of the block. If your gym setup has changed, update it in Settings → Program → Program & Equipment Settings.

I'm hurt. How do I keep a movement out of my workouts?

Open Settings → Program → Program & Equipment Settings and add an entry under Injuries & Limitations. Choose the movement types to avoid (for example overhead pressing, or jumping and landing), how long for (1, 2 or 4 weeks, or until you remove it) and an optional note. Gamut leaves those movements out and checks every workout against them. It is not treatment: follow the advice of whoever is treating you.

I'm taking a day off. Will it hurt my streak?

Tap Make Today a Rest Day on Home's Today card. Resting on your plan's normal rest days never costs your streak, and if a rest day would end it, the card tells you before you rest. A rest day doesn't skip your program ahead: the session that was next is still next tomorrow. Changed your mind? Undo it, or generate anyway.

I forgot to tap Mark Workout Complete

No problem. If you logged at least one set, Gamut finishes the session for you at the end of its day, credits that day to your streak and marks it Auto-saved in your history.

How do I use kilograms, or turn off the automatic rest timer?

Settings → Units switches between pounds and kilograms; prescribed weights are rounded to plates you can actually load. The rest timer starts by itself when you check a working set and shows on your Lock Screen; turn that off in Settings → Workout, or start the timer by hand from the rest time on any exercise.

Apple Health, Apple Watch and widgets

What does Gamut read from Apple Health, and why?

Heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep, active energy, exercise minutes, body weight, VO2 max and recorded workouts. They are used to work out how recovered you are, to ease off the day's loads when your markers say you need it, and to show your strain, stress and energy. Connecting is entirely optional, and the app works fully without it.

Does my health data leave my phone?

Almost never. Health readings are read on your device, cached on your device, and never shared with friends, coaches, teammates or anyone else, or sold. The one exception is body weight: if Apple Health has a weigh-in from today and you haven't logged one yourself, Gamut adds it to your weight log, which is saved to your account like any weigh-in. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

My readiness score says it's calibrating, or I don't see readings

Gamut won't show a score it can't back up. Readiness appears after 3 to 7 days, sooner if your Watch already has a few weeks of history. HRV and resting heart rate comparisons need about a week of nights to build your own baseline. If a reading never appears, check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gamut on your iPhone and turn on anything that is off: iOS never tells an app which permissions you declined.

Sleep heart rate, HRV and blood oxygen are blank after a night's sleep

That usually means your Apple Watch was in Low Power Mode. It still tracks sleep stages and breathing but turns off background heart rate, HRV and blood oxygen, so there is nothing to read. The Health Monitor shows "Not measured last night" when that happens.

How do I use Gamut on my Apple Watch?

Install Gamut on your Watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, and connect Apple Health in Gamut. Then press Start on a workout on your phone and the Watch app opens with it. Each exercise gets its own page: check off sets, change the weight with the Digital Crown, and see your rest countdown and heart rate. It ends the recording when you mark the workout complete. If it doesn't open by itself, open Gamut on your Watch and tap Record Heart Rate.

How do I add a Gamut widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Gamut. There are four: Gamut (today's workout and your streak), Recovery, Stress & Energy and Training Load, and Recovery also fits on the Lock Screen. Coaches also get Team Status and Team Today. Widgets refresh on their own while the app is closed, though iOS pauses that in Low Power Mode, and Health figures can't refresh while your phone is locked. Each widget shows when it was last updated.

Friends and social

How do I add a friend?

Claim a username (you're asked after you sign up, or later in Settings → Account → Choose a Username), then open the Social tab and tap Add a Friend. Search for their exact username, or tap Find Friends from Your Contacts to see which of your contacts use Gamut. That search only finds people who turned on Findable by Email in Settings, and it compares one-way hashes, never a raw email address (how that works). Friend not on Gamut yet? Tap Invite a Friend to send them the app.

What can a friend see about me?

Your username, profile picture, streak, session count, personal-record count, achievement badges, the lift numbers on your Home screen, this week's and this month's sessions and volume, and your progress toward your current goal. Never anything from Apple Health, and never a body-weight number. Once you have a username you also appear on the global leaderboard, which shows only your name, picture and the one figure being ranked.

Can I change my username?

Yes, in Settings → Account → Change Username. Your first change is free; after that you can change it once every 30 days. Your old name is freed for anyone to claim.

What's a High Five, and what's a streak challenge?

A High Five is a quick way for a friend or teammate to cheer a session you completed, with no comment thread. A streak challenge is a race with a friend to a streak length you pick, say 30 days in a row. Whoever gets there first wins; breaking your own streak is a loss for you only. Turn the notifications for each on or off in Settings → Social.

Someone is bothering me on Gamut. What do I do?

Open their profile (or the ⋯ menu on a teammate's card) and tap Block. They immediately lose the ability to see your profile, contact you or challenge you. You can also tap Report to flag them to us; reporting blocks them for you too. For anything else, email support@gamutapp.com.

How do I remove a friend?

Open their profile and tap Remove Friend. This does not block them: they could send you a new request later unless you also block them.

Teams

For players

How do I join my team?

Get your team code (or invite link) from your coach. If you're new, choose With a team, then I'm a player while setting up, and enter the code. Already using Gamut? Go to Settings → Team → Join a Team, or open the invite link. Before you join, Gamut shows exactly what your coaches will see. Players must be 13 or older.

What can my coach see?

A summary of your Gamut training: your lifts and estimated maxes, which days you trained, your streak and records, your training load and effort ratings, the exercises you skipped, which movements you're avoiding, and where you are in your block. Never anything from Apple Health, your body weight or the notes you write. Coaches can't change your program or message you privately. The full list is in the Privacy Policy.

Can I stop sharing with my coach?

Yes, any time, in Settings → Team. Your shared summary is deleted from our servers straight away, and your coaches see only that sharing is off. You can also Leave Team there. Either way your training history stays yours.

What do my teammates see?

Less than your coaches: your name and picture, when you last trained, your streak, your session and record counts, and your latest session so they can High Five it. Never your lifts, goals or training load. Turn off Show Me to Teammates in Settings → Team to hide; you'll stop seeing them too.

My coach set our sport and season. Can I add another sport?

Yes. Your team's sport is set for you, and you can add others in Home's Sports & Seasons editor. Your coach's game days make the day before a game lighter, and on a game day Gamut suggests resting. If your coach put you on a development plan, you keep building through the season instead; Settings → Team says when that's on.

I'm not getting my coach's announcements

Turn on Team Updates in Settings → Team, and allow notifications for Gamut when your iPhone asks. Announcements and notes also show on your Social tab.

What happens if I leave, or the team's plan ends?

Your access through the team ends, you're told in the app, and your coaches stop seeing your training. Everything you logged stays in your account, and you can carry on with your own subscription.

Teams

For coaches

How do I set up my team?
  1. Install Gamut on your iPhone (iOS 26 or later).
  2. Choose With a team, then I'm a coach, and sign up with the email address your team plan was bought with. The plan appears by itself. Used a different email, or Apple's Hide My Email? Enter the claim code from your purchase instead.
  3. Name your team and set its sport and season. Your players' programs are built around them.
How do I add players?

Tap the gear on your dashboard to open team settings. Share your team code, the invite link or the QR code. Players enter it when they join, and their plans start right away. Each player uses one spot on your plan; to free one, open the player and tap Remove from Team. Making a new code stops the old one working; players already on the team stay on.

How do I add assistant coaches?

Share the staff code from team settings. Your assistant installs Gamut, chooses With a team, then I'm a coach, and enters it under Assistant coach?. You can have up to three, and they don't use a player spot.

What do "Overloaded" and "Underprepared" mean?

They're the same lines each player's own app uses. Overloaded means a jump in training load well above that player's usual, or a run of very hard sessions. Underprepared means they've fallen behind their planned sessions or their load has been light. Every Monday you get a weekly report, and you can turn alerts for load spikes, missed sessions and roster changes on or off in team settings.

Can I train with Gamut myself?

Yes. Tap Switch to My Training to use Gamut as an athlete, and Settings → Switch to Coach View to come back. Coaches are covered by the team plan for this too.

How do I change our plan size, update the card or cancel?

Your plan has a billing page where you sign in with the email address it was paid with. From there you can move to a larger size (you pay only the difference), change the card, download invoices or cancel. A plan you cancel stays active to the end of the year already paid for. If you don't have the billing page's link, write to support@gamutapp.com from the address you paid with and we'll send it, or make the change for you.

How does the money-back guarantee work?

Within six months of your team's first payment, email support@gamutapp.com from the address you paid with. We refund everything paid for that first plan to the way you paid, and the plan ends. It covers a team's first plan, not renewals. The details are in the Terms of Use, section 4.5.

What happens if I end the team's program?

End Team Program in team settings tells every player and assistant, and your plan stops covering them; everyone keeps their own history. You can start a new team on the time left on your plan. Ending the program, or deleting your account (which also ends it), doesn't cancel your plan's billing: cancel that as described above.

Your account and data

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or promotions folder for a message from Gamut. On the Log In screen, try signing in: if your email isn't confirmed yet, Gamut offers to Resend Link. Open the link on your iPhone and it brings you back to the app.

I forgot my password

Tap Log In → Forgot your password?. You'll get an email with a link. Open it on the same device you asked from; for security, the link can only be completed there. To change a password you know, use Settings → Account → Change Password.

How do I start over without losing my account?

Settings → Data → Clear Training Data wipes your training history but keeps your login, username, friends and subscription. It can't be undone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings (the gear on the Profile tab) and scroll to the bottom: Account → Delete My Account. Coaches find it at the bottom of team settings. It permanently removes your account, your email address and every workout, set and record in one go, with no recovery and no grace period. It doesn't cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings.

What happens when I sign out?

Gamut saves anything not yet synced, then clears your data from the phone, and you're back at the start. Sign in again and everything comes back from your account. If you're offline with unsynced changes, Gamut asks before throwing them away.

Something looks broken

Email support@gamutapp.com with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps more than anything else.
