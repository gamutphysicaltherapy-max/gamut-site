Support

Answers to the things people ask most, and a way to reach a human.

Last updated 30 September 2026

Email support@gamutapp.com and you will get a reply within two business days. Include your account email and, if something went wrong, roughly when it happened.

Subscription

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 14 days free. You start the trial at the end of setup by choosing a monthly or yearly plan; Apple asks you to confirm with your Apple ID, but nothing is charged for the first 14 days. When the trial ends, the plan you chose begins and is billed by Apple — unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, in which case you are never charged. The trial is available once per Apple ID. Your training history stays exactly where it is either way.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so they are cancelled in iOS rather than in Gamut:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Gamut, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep access until the period you have already paid for runs out. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

I was charged but the app still asks me to subscribe

Open Gamut, go to the subscription screen and tap Restore purchases. That asks the App Store to replay your purchase to the app. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it still does not appear, email us.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — we have no ability to issue them. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apple Health

What does Gamut read, and why?

Heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, body weight, VO2 max and recorded workouts. They are used to work out how recovered you are and to ease off the day's loads when your markers say you need it. Connecting is entirely optional and the app works fully without it.

Does my health data leave my phone?

Almost never. Health readings are read on your device, cached on your device, and never shared with friends or other users, or sold. The one exception is body weight: if Apple Health has a weigh-in from today and you haven't logged one yourself, Gamut adds it to your weight log, which is saved to your account like any weigh-in. See the Privacy Policy for the detail.

I connected it but I do not see any readings

Two common reasons. First, Gamut needs about two weeks of data before it will show a comparison — a baseline built from fewer readings is noise, so it says it is still building rather than showing you something misleading. Second, iOS never tells an app which health permissions you granted, only that you were asked. If you declined a category in the permission sheet, the app cannot tell — check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gamut and turn on anything that is off.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

In Gamut, go to Profile → Settings → Delete My Account. This permanently removes your account, your email address and every workout, set and record, in one operation. There is no recovery and no grace period.

How do I start over without losing my account?

Profile → Settings → Clear Training Data wipes your training history but keeps your login. It does not affect your subscription or free trial.

I forgot my password

Tap Log in → Forgot password. You will be emailed a link. Open it on the same device you requested it from — for security, the link can only be completed on that device.

Friends & Social

How do I add a friend?

Claim a username (you'll be asked right after you sign up, or later from Profile → Settings), then open the Social tab. Search for an exact username, or tap Find Friends from Your Contacts to see which of your phone contacts already use Gamut — this only compares one-way hashes of email addresses, never a raw email or contact detail; see the Privacy Policy for how that works. Send a request, and once they accept, you're friends.

What can a friend see about me?

Your username, streak, session count, personal-record count, achievement badges, lift numbers you've logged, and your progress toward your current goal. Never anything read from Apple Health, and never a raw body-weight number.

What's a "High Five"?

A quick way for a friend to acknowledge a session you completed, without a comment thread. Turn High Five notifications on or off in Profile → Settings → Notifications.

What's a streak challenge?

A race with a friend to a target streak length you both pick — say, 30 days in a row. Whoever reaches it first wins. Breaking your own streak before then is a loss for you only; it has no effect on your friend's side of the race.

Someone is bothering me on Gamut. What do I do?

Open their profile and tap Block — they immediately lose the ability to see your profile, contact you, or challenge you. You can also tap Report to flag their account to us; blocking and reporting are independent, so you can do either or both. For anything else, email support@gamutapp.com.

How do I remove a friend?

Open their profile and tap Remove Friend. This does not block them — they could send you a new request later unless you also block them.

Training

Why can I only generate one workout a day?

Because a training block is a sequence, not a menu. Each day is built from where you are in the block, what you did recently and how you rated it — generating several at once would mean guessing at ratings that do not exist yet.

The weights look wrong for a lift I have never done

For a main lift with no logged history, Gamut estimates a starting weight from conventional strength ratios and deliberately biases it low, because on an unfamiliar lift the limiter is usually technique. Log a set and rate it — if it was easy, the app steps the number up next session, and once you have real logged sets it uses those instead of the estimate.

Something looks broken

Email support@gamutapp.com with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps more than anything else.
