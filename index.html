Gamut Automated strength programming

A new workout, built for you, every training day.

Gamut is a strength and conditioning app that writes your program day by day — periodized around your sport's season, loaded off your own logged sets, and eased back when your recovery says to.

What it does

  • Programs the whole block. Four, six or eight weeks of undulating periodization, with deloads scheduled where they belong and intensity that climbs across each wave.
  • Loads off your real numbers. Estimated one-rep maxes averaged across five formulas from the sets you actually logged, with a safety factor applied before anything is prescribed.
  • Follows your season. Track several sports at once; block length and emphasis shift as you move between off-season, pre-season and in-season.
  • Reads your recovery. Optionally connects to Apple Health for heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep, and eases the day's loads when your markers are down.
  • Designed by a clinician. Built by a Doctor of Physical Therapy who is also a Certified Strength and Conditioning Specialist.

One month free. Every new account gets full access for a month with no payment details required. After that Gamut is an auto-renewing subscription through the App Store.

Privacy, briefly

No advertising. No analytics SDK. No trackers. Apple Health data is read on your device and stays there — apart from a same-day weigh-in, which is added to your weight log. Your account and training history can be deleted permanently from inside the app, in one tap. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
