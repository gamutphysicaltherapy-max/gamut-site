How it works

Four things a template can't do.

Swipe

01 · Your numbers

Your numbers, every set.

Weights come from the sets you log, with a warm-up built in. Rate how hard it felt, and the next day adjusts.

02 · Your season

Your season, planned.

Build in the off-season. Peak before games start. Hold your strength in season, with lighter weeks to rest.

03 · Your recovery

Your recovery, read.

Connect Apple Health, and the day gets lighter when your sleep and heart rate say you need it.

04 · Your people

Your streak, your friends.

Keep a streak going and earn badges. Add your friends, send a High Five, and see who trained the most this week.
