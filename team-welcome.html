Set up your team

  1. Download Gamut on your iPhone.
  2. Choose With a team, then I'm a coach, and sign up with the email you paid with. Your plan appears automatically. If you sign up with a different email (or with Sign in with Apple's hidden email), enter the claim code above instead.
  3. Name your team, then share its team code with your players. Each player chooses With a team, then I'm a player, and enters it.

A receipt is on its way to your email. Keep this page's claim code until your team is set up. Questions? support@gamutapp.com
