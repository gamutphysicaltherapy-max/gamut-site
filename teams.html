The problem

One team lift can't fit every player.

  • Stronger teams push your players around.
  • You can't see who trains in the off-season.
  • You can't tell who is doing too much until it shows.

Gamut fixes that. Here's how →

How it works

You coach. Gamut writes each plan.

1 · Each player

A plan for every player.

You set the sport and the season once, for the whole team. Each player adds their goals and gear. Gamut makes their workout each day. It sets the lifts, sets, reps and weights.

  • Weights go up as they get stronger.
  • Speed days with sprints and jumps.
  • A warm-up every time, and light weeks to rest.

2 · Your team

See your whole team.

Each player gets a tag: on track, doing too much, or falling behind.

  • Watch each player's lifts go up.
  • A team report every Monday.
  • A heads-up when a player does too much or skips.
  • Send a note or a High Five.

    Questions

    What coaches ask.

    Do players need an iPhone?

    Yes. Gamut is an iPhone app, for iOS 26 or later. You use it on your iPhone too.

    Does it work in season?

    Yes. Add your season and game days. In season, each plan keeps strength and power with less volume, and the day before a game is lighter.

    What can I see?

    Their lifts, the workouts they did, how hard it felt and their records. Never their health data, body weight or private notes. Players say yes to sharing when they join. They can turn it off, and you'll see that.

    How old do players need to be?

    13 or older.

    What if a player leaves?

    Take them off your team. Their spot opens up.

    Can I change or cancel?

    Move up a size any time. You only pay the difference. A smaller size starts next year. Cancel any time. Your plan lasts to the end of the year you paid for.
