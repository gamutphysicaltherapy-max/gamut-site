Gamut for Teams Automated strength programming for your whole roster

Every player on a real program. You see how it's going.

Gamut builds each player their own periodized strength and conditioning program, fitted to your sport's season, and gives you a dashboard of their training.

What your team gets

  • A program for every player. Built around their sport, their season, their equipment and their own logged lifts — adjusted session by session.
  • A coach dashboard. Each player's lifts and estimated maxes, sessions completed against plan, training load and effort, and who has fallen behind.
  • Alerts that matter. A heads-up when a player's training load spikes or they miss sessions.
  • Up to three assistant coaches, who don't use a player spot.
  • Privacy built in. Players agree to share their training when they join, and can turn sharing off. Coaches never see Apple Health data, body weight or private notes.

Paying by purchase order or invoice? Email support@gamutapp.com with your school, roster size and billing contact. We'll send a quote, and you can pay the invoice by card or bank transfer on net terms.

After you buy

  1. Download Gamut on your iPhone.
  2. Choose With a team, then I'm a coach, and sign up with the same email you used to pay. Your plan appears automatically (or enter the claim code from your confirmation page).
  3. Name your team and share its team code with your players.

Manage your plan

Change your plan size, update your card, download invoices or cancel at the billing portal (sign in with the email you paid with).
