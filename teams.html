How it works

You coach the team. Gamut writes every player's program.

1 · Every player

A real program for every player, built fresh each day.

Each player tells Gamut their experience, their goals and the equipment they have. It builds their workout every day: the exercises, sets, reps, weights and warm-up.

  • Weights adjust from what they log and how hard it felt.
  • Speed and power days, with sprint and jump work.
  • A warm-up every session, and planned lighter weeks to recover.
  • 30 to 90 minutes, in a school weight room, a garage or with bodyweight.

2 · Your dashboard

See your whole team at a glance.

Every player gets a status: on track, overloaded or underprepared. It comes from their own training load and the sessions they've done against their plan.

  • Each player's lifts and estimated maxes, week by week.
  • A weekly report every Monday, a team PR feed and a strength board.
  • Alerts when a player's load spikes or they miss sessions.
  • Send a note or a High Five, or post an announcement to the team.

3 · Your season

Built around your season, not a calendar.

Set your sport and season once. Every player's plan follows it: build in the off-season, sharpen in pre-season, hold strength during the season.

  • Put games on the schedule, and the day before lightens up.
  • Players who won't see much game time can keep building on a development plan.
  • Players who play other sports can add them, and their plan covers both.

Everything included

Every plan includes all of it.

No add-ons and no feature tiers. The only thing that changes is how many players you bring.

Questions

What coaches ask before they buy.

Do players need an iPhone?

Yes. Gamut is an iPhone app, for iOS 26 and later, and you run the coach dashboard on your iPhone too.

Does it replace my program?

It handles each player's individual strength and speed work, planned around your season. You stay the coach: you set the sport, season and schedule, and you see how every player is doing.

What can I see about my players?

Their lifts and estimated maxes, sessions done against their plan, training load, effort ratings and PRs. Never their Apple Health data, body weight or private notes. Players agree to share when they join and can turn sharing off, and you'll see that it's off.

How old do players need to be?

13 or older.

What if a player leaves the team?

Remove them from your roster and their spot opens up for someone else.

What if it doesn't work for us?

You're covered by our 6-month money-back guarantee. If your players aren't getting stronger and faster, or you're not happy for any reason, email us within 6 months of your first payment for a full refund. No questions asked.

Can I change sizes or cancel?

Move up a size any time and pay only the difference for the rest of the year. A smaller size starts at renewal. Cancel any time in the billing portal, and your plan runs to the end of the year you paid for.
