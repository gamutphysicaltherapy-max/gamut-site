Privacy Policy

What Gamut collects, where it goes, and what never leaves your phone.

Last updated 16 October 2026

Gamut is a training app that builds a new workout for you each day and adjusts it as you log your sets. This policy describes what data that requires, who processes it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the Gamut iOS app, its Apple Watch app and widgets, and this website.

The short version. Gamut stores your account and your training history so it survives a new phone. Apple Health data is read on your device and stays there — it is never shared with other users and never sold — with one exception you should know about: if a body-weight reading from Apple Health is the only weigh-in for today, it is added to your Gamut weight log, which is saved to your account like a weigh-in you type yourself. If you join a team, your coaches see a summary of your Gamut training (never anything from Apple Health, never your body weight), and you can turn that sharing off at any time. Nothing you do in Gamut is sold or used for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app.

Who is responsible

Gamut is operated by the developer of the Gamut app ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, or to make a privacy request, contact support@gamutapp.com.

What we collect

Account information

An email address, and either a password or a sign-in through Apple or Google, so your training survives reinstalling the app or switching phones. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider and are stored only as a salted hash — we never see, store or have any way to recover your password. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider tells us your email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") and may share your name; we never receive your Apple or Google password.

Your training profile

The answers you give during setup and in the app: your name, age, height, body weight, training experience, the Olympic lifts you have been coached on, the sports you play and their seasons, your goal and target, your training days per week, the equipment you have access to, your session length, and your preferences (units, notification settings, rest days you mark). This is what makes a generated workout specific to you rather than generic.

Injuries and limitations. If you add one, we store which movement types to avoid (for example "overhead pressing"), how long the entry lasts, and an optional short note you write. Because this can describe an injury, keep the note to what matters for training and leave out anything you would not want stored.

Your training records

Every set you log — the exercise, weight, reps and your effort rating — plus which workouts you completed or skipped, your streak, your weigh-ins, your goal measurements, and your generated workout history. This is the app's core function; without it there is no progression, no estimated maxes and no streak.

Apple Health data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Gamut reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep, active energy, exercise minutes, body weight, VO2 max and recorded workouts. If you use the Gamut Apple Watch app, it saves your strength-training workouts (with heart rate and active energy) to Apple Health. All of this is optional, the app works fully without it, and you can turn it off at any time in the iOS Health app.

Apple Health readings stay on your device. They are stored in Gamut's local cache on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers, not shown to friends, coaches or other users, not shared with any third party, not used for advertising or marketing, and not sold — ever, to anyone, for any purpose.

The one exception is body weight. If Apple Health has a body-weight reading from today and you have not logged a weigh-in yourself today, Gamut adds it to your weight log and profile, which are saved to your account and used exactly like a weigh-in you type — to chart progress toward a weight goal. Friends and coaches never see a body-weight number. You can avoid this by not granting Gamut read access to Body Weight in the Health app.

Health readings do influence your training on-device: your recovery score and the load adjustment for a session are calculated on your phone from those readings. When a workout is generated, only the resulting training numbers are sent — an intensity percentage and your estimated training maxes — never a heart rate, a sleep duration or any other health measurement.

Profile picture (optional)

If you add a profile picture, Gamut opens your photo library so you can pick one photo; only the photo you pick is used, and it is cropped and resized on your phone before upload. It is stored at a web address that is not listed anywhere but can be viewed by anyone who has the link, and it is shown to your friends and beside your name on leaderboards. Replacing or removing it, or deleting your account, removes it.

Contacts (optional, to find friends)

If you choose to use "Find Friends from Your Contacts" on the Social tab, Gamut reads the email addresses in your device's contacts on your device and converts each one to a one-way cryptographic hash before anything leaves your phone. Only those hashes are sent to our servers, which check them against hashes of the email addresses already registered to Gamut accounts and return matching accounts — never a raw email address, in either direction, and never a contact's name, phone number or any other contact detail. This feature is entirely optional and only runs when you tap it.

Your contact list itself never reaches our servers. Once you claim a username, and only if your email address has been verified, we store a hash of your own account's email address so other Gamut users who already have your email in their contacts can find you. This is on by default and can be turned off with "Findable by Email" in Settings, which removes you from those searches. We cannot reverse a hash back into an email address, and we do not use this feature's data for advertising, marketing or any purpose other than showing people which of their contacts already use Gamut.

Friends

If you claim a username, other Gamut users can find your account by exact username (or by email, as described above) and send a friend request. Once you accept, a friend can see your username, display name and profile picture; your streak, session count, personal-record count and achievement badges; your sessions and volume this week and this month; whether you trained today; the lift numbers shown on your Home screen; your progress toward your current goal; and a short summary of each completed session (its date and type, and how many exercises, sets and personal records it had, and the total weight moved). They never see anything from Apple Health and never a body-weight number. Friends can send you a "High Five" on a completed session, and you can challenge a friend to a streak race; that friend can see the target you are racing to and whether you are still racing, have reached it, or broke your streak first.

If you turn on push notifications for these features, we register a push notification token for your device so a friend request, High Five, challenge update or (if you turn it on) a friend's personal record can reach you when the app is closed. A notification about a friend's record names the friend and nothing else — never the lift or the number. The token is used for nothing else and is removed when you sign out.

You can remove a friend, block a user or report a user at any time. A report stores who reported whom, the reason you choose and any detail you add, so we can review it; reporting also blocks that user for you immediately.

Global leaderboard

Once you have claimed a username, you appear on Gamut's global leaderboard, which every Gamut user can see, not only your friends. For each ranking it shows your username, display name, profile picture and the one figure being ranked — your sessions or total weight moved this week or this month, your streak, or your number of personal records — and your position. Nothing else from your profile or history is shown there. People you have blocked, and people who have blocked you, do not see each other on it.

Teams: if you join one as a player

You join a team only by entering a code from its coach, and only after agreeing, on a screen that lists exactly what is shared, to share your training with that team's coaches (its head coach and up to three assistant coaches). While sharing is on, your coaches can see: the name you give when joining; when you last trained, your sessions completed against your plan, your streak and your personal-record count; your estimated maxes on your main lifts and how they have trended; your training load from Gamut workouts (your effort rating multiplied by the session's length, by week) and your average effort rating; your current training block, week and season phase; a summary of your recent sessions (the date, the workout's title, how many sets you did, the total weight moved, your effort rating and any exercise you skipped); and which types of movement you are avoiding because of an injury or limitation (for example "overhead pressing").

Coaches never see anything from Apple Health (no heart rate, HRV, sleep, recovery or readiness), your body weight, the note you wrote about an injury, or any other note you write for yourself. Coaches cannot change your program, and they cannot message you through Gamut.

Sharing can be turned off at any time in Settings → Team. When you turn it off, the summary your coaches could see is deleted from our servers straight away and nothing more is sent; your coaches see only that your sharing is off. You can also leave the team at any time. Your coaches may receive an alert when you join or leave, when you turn sharing off, when your training load this week is well above your usual, or when you have fallen behind on your planned sessions; an alert names you and says which of these happened, and nothing more.

A coach can remove you from the roster or end the team's program. Either way you are told in the app, your coaches stop seeing your training, and your own training history stays in your account.

Teams: if you are a coach

We store your team's name and sport, the name you give your players, your team's join codes, which alerts you have turned on, and your team plan (how many players it covers, when it is paid through, and the email address it was bought with, which is how the plan is matched to your account). Your players see your team's name and their coaches' names, never your account email. What you can see about your players is described above and is visible only to your team's coaching staff.

Team plans bought on our website

Team plans are bought on our website through Stripe, and sold through Link, Stripe's payment service, which acts as the seller of record and calculates and collects sales tax. At checkout, Stripe collects your payment details, email address, billing address and, if you give them, your organization's name and tax ID. We never receive your card or bank details. Stripe sends us the purchaser's email address, which plan was bought, whether it is active and when it renews or ends, and Stripe's own identifiers for the customer and the subscription. Stripe's handling of your purchase is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy and Link's Privacy Policy.

Your subscription

When you subscribe or start a free trial, Apple gives your device a signed record of the purchase. We send that record to our server to verify it with Apple's signature, and store the product you chose, when your current period (or free trial) ends, the transaction's identifier and whether it was a test purchase — so the service can confirm your subscription. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No payment details. Individual subscriptions are billed by Apple through your Apple ID, and team plans by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number, bank details or Apple ID.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers. Gamut contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no third-party trackers, and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). It never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.
  • No location, microphone or camera access. The app requests none of these permissions. It uses your photo library only when you choose a profile picture, and your contacts only when you use contact matching — both optional, and the app works fully without either.
  • Training reminders are local. Workout reminders, streak alerts, recovery tips, the weekly recap and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled entirely on your device — nothing is sent from a server for these. Only the Friends and Teams notifications described above are sent from our server.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each one only receives what its function requires.

Supabase

Hosts our database, file storage and authentication. Your account, training profile, logged sets, workout history, subscription record and profile picture are stored there. Database rules make each account's rows readable only by that account, except for what you share with friends, on the leaderboard and with your team's coaches as described above. Data is held on servers in the United States.

Anthropic

Generates the exercise selection for each workout. When you generate a day, we send: your goal and training focus (including the lift your goal names, if any), the sports you listed, your season phase, your age, your experience level and the Olympic lifts you've been coached on, your available equipment and preferred exercises, your session length, your unit preference, any active injuries and limitations (the movement types to avoid and your note), your target intensity for the day, your estimated training maxes, and from your last few sessions the exercise names, your effort ratings and any exercises you skipped.

Your name, email address, body weight and Apple Health data are not sent. The request is not used to train any model.

Expo

Delivers Friends and Teams push notifications to Apple's notification service. It receives your device's push token and the notification's text (for example, who sent you a High Five, or that a player joined your team), and nothing else.

Stripe and Link

Process team plan payments and calculate sales tax, as described in "Team plans bought on our website". They do not receive any of your training data.

Apple

Distributes the app, processes subscription payments and provides Sign in with Apple. Apple's handling of your purchase and sign-in is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google

Provides Sign in with Google, if you use it. Google's handling of that sign-in is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Where your data is stored on your device

Your training data is kept on your phone so the app works without a connection — which matters in a gym with no signal. Your login session is stored separately in the iOS Keychain rather than in ordinary app storage, because a session token is a credential rather than app data. The Home Screen widget and the rest-timer Live Activity read a small summary kept on your phone; nothing extra is sent anywhere to show them.

How long we keep it

Your training data is kept for as long as your account exists, because its entire value is being a long record. Delete your account and it is removed immediately and permanently. The summary your coaches see is deleted when you turn sharing off, leave or are removed from the team, or the team's program ends.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear Training Data — in Settings, wipes every workout, set and record while keeping your account, username, friends and profile picture. Irreversible.
  • Delete My Account — in Settings, permanently deletes your account, your email address, your profile picture, your username and friends, and every row of training data in one operation. There is no recovery and no grace period. It does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. If you are a team's head coach, deleting your account also ends the team's program, and its players and assistant coaches are told.
  • Remove your profile picture — on the Profile tab, at any time.
  • Findable by Email — in Settings, turn off to stop other users' contact searches from finding you.
  • Team sharing — in Settings → Team, turn off sharing with your coaches, or leave the team. Coaches turn their alerts on or off in their team settings.
  • Notifications — each kind can be turned on or off in Settings.
  • Disconnect Apple Health — in the iOS Health app under Sharing → Apps → Gamut, turn off the categories you no longer want Gamut to read. Readings already cached on your phone are deleted when you sign out, switch accounts or delete the app.
  • Revoke Contacts or Photos access — in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Revoking Contacts stops future contact searches from being run by you; it does not undo a match that already happened.
  • Access and correction — your profile is editable in the app. For a copy of your data in a portable format, email us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR (EU/UK) or the CCPA (California), including access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA. To exercise any right, email support@gamutapp.com.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS. Database access is restricted per account by row-level security policies enforced by the database itself. Your login session is stored in the iOS Keychain. No credential in the app can read or modify another account's private data.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and any regulator required by law.

Children

Gamut is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, the app does not let you set up a profile with an age under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because the app prescribes resistance training loads, users under 16 should train under qualified supervision, such as a coach's. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Gamut from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed there, under appropriate safeguards for transfers out of the EEA and UK.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
