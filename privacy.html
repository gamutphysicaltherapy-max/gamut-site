The short version

Gamut stores your account and your training history so it survives a new phone. Apple Health data is read on your device and stays there. It is never shared with other users and never sold, with one exception you should know about: if a body-weight reading from Apple Health is the only weigh-in for today, it is added to your Gamut weight log, which is saved to your account like a weigh-in you type yourself.

Progress photos you add never leave your phone, and nor does your calendar: Gamut can add your training days to it, but never reads the events already there.

If you join a team, your coaches see a summary of your Gamut training (never anything from Apple Health, never your body weight), and you can turn that sharing off at any time. Nothing you do in Gamut is sold or used for advertising, and there is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app.

Who is responsible

Gamut is operated by the developer of the Gamut app ("we", "us"). For any question about this policy, or to make a privacy request, contact support@gamutapp.com.

What we collect

Account information

An email address, and either a password or a sign-in through Apple or Google, so your training survives reinstalling the app or switching phones. Passwords are handled entirely by our authentication provider and are stored only as a salted hash. We never see, store or have any way to recover your password. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that provider tells us your email address (with Apple, this can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email") and may share your name; we never receive your Apple or Google password. We email you only about your account: to confirm your address and to reset your password.

Your training profile

The answers you give during setup and in the app: your name, age, height, body weight, training experience, the Olympic lifts you have been coached on, the sports you play and their seasons, your goal and target, your training days per week, the equipment you have access to, your session length, and your preferences (units, notification settings, rest days you mark). This is what makes a generated workout specific to you rather than generic.

Injuries and limitations. If you add one, we store which movement types to avoid (for example "overhead pressing"), how long the entry lasts, and an optional short note you write. Because this can describe an injury, keep the note to what matters for training and leave out anything you would not want stored.

Your training records

Every set you log (the exercise, weight, reps and your effort rating), plus which workouts you completed or skipped, how hard each session felt, your streak, your weigh-ins, your goal measurements, and your generated workout history. This is the app's core function; without it there is no progression, no estimated maxes and no streak.

Body measurements (optional). If you log them, we store your waist, chest, hip, upper-arm and thigh measurements and body-fat percentage, with the date of each entry, so they survive a new phone. They are shown only to you: never to friends, teammates, coaches or on any leaderboard, and never sent to the workout generator.

Profile picture (optional)

If you add a profile picture, Gamut opens your photo library so you can pick one photo, or your camera so you can take one; only that photo is used, and it is cropped and resized on your phone before upload. It is stored at a web address that is not listed anywhere but can be viewed by anyone who has the link, and it is shown to your friends and teammates and beside your name on leaderboards. Replacing or removing it, or deleting your account, removes it.

Progress photos (optional)

If you add progress photos, Gamut takes them with your camera or copies the ones you pick from your library into its own storage on your phone, resized. They are never uploaded to our servers, never shown to anyone else, and never sent to any service provider. They are kept for the account that added them, so another person who signs in on the same phone does not see them. They are not synced, so they do not move to a new phone, though your phone's own backup may include them. You can delete any photo in the app, and Delete My Account removes them all from that phone.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on "Workouts in Your Calendar" in Settings, Gamut asks for calendar access, shows you the calendars on your phone (their names and the account each belongs to, such as iCloud, Google or Outlook) so you can pick one, and adds your next two weeks of training days to it. Each event holds the training focus for that day, a time from your reminder settings, and a link back into Gamut. Gamut never reads, changes or removes any event it did not add, and nothing from your calendar is sent to our servers. If the calendar you pick syncs to an online account, its provider receives those events under its own privacy policy. Turning the feature off, signing out or deleting your account removes the events Gamut added.

Contacts (optional, to find friends)

If you choose to use "Find Friends from Your Contacts" on the Social tab, Gamut reads the email addresses in your device's contacts on your device and converts each one to a one-way cryptographic hash before anything leaves your phone. Only those hashes are sent to our servers, which check them against the hashes of people who have chosen to be findable and return matching accounts. Never a raw email address, in either direction, and never a contact's name, phone number or any other contact detail. This feature only runs when you tap it, and the number of searches is limited.

Being findable is off until you turn it on. Your contact list itself never reaches our servers. If you turn on "Findable by Email" in Settings, and only if your email address has been verified, we store a hash of your own account's email address so people who already have your email in their contacts can find you. Turning it off removes the hash. We cannot reverse a hash back into an email address, and we use it for nothing except showing people which of their contacts already use Gamut.

Home Screen widgets

Gamut's widgets show a summary that the app keeps on your phone. So they stay current while the app is closed, a phone with a Gamut widget on it registers a push token with us (without asking for notification permission, which a silent update does not need) and we send it a silent, content-free signal about once an hour that wakes Gamut to refresh the widget on the phone. We store that the device has a widget and when it was last refreshed. This stops on its own a few days after you remove the widgets, and the token is removed when you sign out.

Apple Health data

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Gamut reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate recovery, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, sleep, active energy, exercise minutes, body weight, VO2 max and recorded workouts, and, from your Health profile, your biological sex, which is used only to compare your VO2 max with people of your age and sex. If you use the Gamut Apple Watch app, it saves your strength-training workouts (with heart rate and active energy) to Apple Health. All of this is optional, the app works fully without it, and you can turn it off at any time in the iOS Health app.

Apple Health readings stay on your device. They are stored in Gamut's local cache on your phone. They are not uploaded to our servers, not shown to friends, coaches, teammates or other users, not shared with any third party, not used for advertising or marketing, and not sold. Ever, to anyone, for any purpose.

The one exception is body weight. If Apple Health has a body-weight reading from today and you have not logged a weigh-in yourself today, Gamut adds it to your weight log and profile, which are saved to your account and used exactly like a weigh-in you type: to chart progress toward a weight goal. Friends and coaches never see a body-weight number. You can avoid this by not granting Gamut read access to Body Weight in the Health app.

Health readings do influence your training on your phone: your recovery score and the load adjustment for a session are calculated there from those readings. When a workout is generated, only the resulting training numbers are sent (an intensity percentage and your estimated training maxes), never a heart rate, a sleep duration or any other health measurement.

Friends and the leaderboard

Friends

If you claim a username, other Gamut users can find your account by exact username (or by email, if you have turned that on) and send a friend request. Once you accept, a friend can see your username, display name and profile picture; your streak, session count, personal-record count and achievement badges; your sessions and volume this week and this month; whether you trained today; the lift numbers shown on your Home screen; your progress toward your current goal; and a short summary of each completed session (its date and type, and how many exercises, sets and personal records it had, and the total weight moved). They never see anything from Apple Health and never a body-weight number. Friends can send you a "High Five" on a completed session, and you can challenge a friend to a streak race; that friend can see the target you are racing to and whether you are still racing, have reached it, or broke your streak first.

If you turn on push notifications for these features, we register a push notification token for your device so a friend request, High Five, challenge update or (if you turn it on) a friend's personal record can reach you when the app is closed. A notification about a friend's record names the friend and nothing else, never the lift or the number. The token is removed when you sign out.

You can remove a friend, block a user or report a user at any time. A report stores who reported whom, the reason you choose and any detail you add, so we can review it; reporting also blocks that user for you immediately.

Global leaderboard

Once you have claimed a username, you appear on Gamut's global leaderboard, which every Gamut user can see, not only your friends. For each ranking it shows your username, display name, profile picture and the one figure being ranked (your sessions or total weight moved this week or this month, your streak, or your number of personal records) and your position. Nothing else from your profile or history is shown there. People you have blocked, and people who have blocked you, do not see each other on it.

Groups

You can join groups that each have their own leaderboard: your state (which you pick; Gamut does not read your location), your age group (matched to the age on your profile, so the 13–17 group contains only athletes who gave an age from 13 to 17), and groups other athletes create. A group's leaderboard shows its members the same things the global leaderboard does, and only members can see it. If you create a group, its name is shown to anyone who searches for groups, and we keep that you created it. Group names are checked against a list of words that aren't allowed before anyone sees them. Anyone can report a group; a report stores who reported it and the reason they chose, and a group reported by several people is hidden until we review it. Leaving a group removes you from it straight away, and deleting your account removes your memberships and any group you created stays without your name attached.

Teams

If you join a team as a player

You join a team only by entering a code from its coach (or opening their invite link), and only after agreeing, on a screen that lists exactly what is shared, to share your training with that team's coaches: its head coach and up to three assistant coaches.

While sharing is on, coaches see

  • The name you give when joining
  • When you last trained, which days you trained over the last five weeks, sessions completed against your plan, and your streak
  • Your personal-record count and recent records (the lift, weight, reps and date)
  • Your estimated maxes on your main lifts and how they have trended
  • Your training load from Gamut workouts (effort rating times session length, by week, and your last 7 days against your usual week) and your average effort rating
  • Your current block, week and season phase, and whether this is a planned lighter week
  • A summary of recent sessions: date, title, sets done, total weight moved, effort rating and any exercise you skipped
  • Which types of movement you are avoiding because of an injury (for example "overhead pressing")

Coaches never see

  • Anything from Apple Health: no heart rate, HRV, sleep, recovery or readiness
  • Your body weight
  • The note you wrote about an injury
  • Any other note you write for yourself

Coaches cannot change your program or send you private messages.

What your coaches can send you. Your coaches can post short announcements to the team, send you one of a fixed set of notes (for example "Great week of training. Keep it going."), give your sessions a High Five, and keep a team schedule of games, practices and off days. You can't reply to any of these; there is no messaging. The team's sport, season and schedule shape your training on your own phone: on a game day the app suggests resting, and the day before a game your session is lighter. A coach can also put you on a development plan (for a player not expected to play much that season): your program then keeps building through the season, game days don't change your training, and you are told, with the plan shown in Settings → Team. You can report an announcement from the app. You get notifications for announcements and notes only if you turn on "Team Updates" in Settings → Team.

Your teammates (other players on the same team) see less than your coaches: the name you give when joining and your profile picture; when you last trained, your current streak, your number of sessions and personal records, and your sessions this week; and your latest session (the type of day, its date and how many exercises), so they can send you a High Five. Teammates never see your lifts, goals, training load, recent-session details or anything from Apple Health. If you turn on "Teammate PRs", a teammate's new record sends you a notification naming them and nothing else. You can turn teammate visibility off in Settings → Team ("Show Me to Teammates"); while it is off, you are hidden from your teammates and they are hidden from you. Blocking a teammate hides you from each other.

Sharing can be turned off at any time in Settings → Team. When you turn it off, the summary your coaches could see is deleted from our servers straight away and nothing more is sent; your coaches see only that your sharing is off. You can also leave the team at any time. Your coaches may receive an alert when you join or leave, when you turn sharing off, when your training load this week is well above your usual, or when you have fallen behind on your planned sessions; an alert names you and says which of these happened, and nothing more.

A coach can remove you from the roster or end the team's program. Either way you are told in the app, your coaches stop seeing your training, and your own training history stays in your account.

If you are a coach

We store your team's name, sport and season, the name you give your players, your team's join codes, which alerts you have turned on, your team's schedule, the announcements and notes you send (with your name on them), how you have arranged your roster (position groups, starters and order, visible only to your coaching staff), which players you have put on a development plan (each of those players is told), and your team plan (how many players it covers, when it is paid through, and the email address it was bought with, which is how the plan is matched to your account). Your players see your team's name and their coaches' names, never your account email. What you can see about your players is described above and is visible only to your team's coaching staff. The coach widgets on your Lock Screen show counts only, never a player's name.

Subscriptions and team plans

Your subscription

When you subscribe or start a free trial, Apple gives your device a signed record of the purchase. We send that record to our server to verify it with Apple's signature, and store the product you chose, when your current period (or free trial) ends, the transaction's identifier and whether it was a test purchase, so the service can confirm your subscription. A new purchase is tagged with your Gamut account's random identifier, which Apple keeps with the purchase, so that a subscription unlocks only the account that bought it. We never receive your payment details or your Apple ID.

Team plans

Team plans are bought outside the app through Stripe, and sold through Link, Stripe's payment service, which acts as the seller of record and calculates and collects sales tax. At checkout, Stripe collects your payment details, email address, billing address and, if you give them, your organization's name and tax ID. We never receive your card or bank details. Stripe sends us the purchaser's email address, which plan was bought, whether it is active and when it renews or ends, and Stripe's own identifiers for the customer and the subscription. If we invite a coach to a free trial team plan, we store the email address the invitation was sent to. Stripe's handling of your purchase is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy and Link's Privacy Policy.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No payment details. Individual subscriptions are billed by Apple through your Apple ID, and team plans by Stripe. We never receive or store your card number, bank details or Apple ID.
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers. Gamut contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no third-party trackers, and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). It never presents the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.
  • No location or microphone access. The app requests neither, and your state group is one you pick, not one we work out. It uses your camera and photo library only when you take or choose a profile picture or progress photo, your calendars only if you turn on calendar sync, and your contacts only when you use contact matching. All of these are optional, and the app works fully without any of them.
  • Training reminders are local. Workout reminders, streak alerts, recovery tips, the weekly recap and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled entirely on your device; nothing is sent from a server for these. Only the Friends and Teams notifications described above, and the silent widget refresh, are sent from our server.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of service providers. Each one only receives what its function requires.

Supabase

Hosts our database, file storage and authentication. Your account, training profile, logged sets, workout history, subscription record and profile picture are stored there, and so are messages sent through this website's question form. Database rules make each account's rows readable only by that account, except for what you share with friends, teammates, on the leaderboards (global and group) and with your team's coaches as described above. Progress photos and your calendar are never stored there. Data is held on servers in the United States.

Anthropic

Generates the exercise selection for each workout. When you generate a day, we send: your goal and training focus (including the lift your goal names, if any), the sports you listed, your season phase, your age, your experience level and the Olympic lifts you've been coached on, your available equipment, training setting and preferred exercises, your session length and training days per week, your unit preference, any active injuries and limitations (the movement types to avoid and your note), your target intensity for the day, your estimated training maxes, how much you have already trained this week, and from your last few sessions the exercise names, your effort ratings and any exercises you skipped.

Your name, email address, body weight, body measurements, photos and Apple Health data are not sent. The request is not used to train any model.

Expo

Delivers Friends and Teams push notifications, and the silent widget refresh, to Apple's notification service. It receives your device's push token and the notification's text (for example, who sent you a High Five, or that a player joined your team), and nothing else.

Stripe and Link

Process team plan payments and calculate sales tax, as described in "Team plans". They do not receive any of your training data.

Apple

Distributes the app, processes subscription payments and provides Sign in with Apple. Apple's handling of your purchase and sign-in is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google

Provides Sign in with Google, if you use it, and sends our account emails (address confirmation and password reset) through Gmail, so it receives your email address and the content of those messages. Google's handling of both is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

GitHub

Hosts this website. See "This website" below.

This website

This website sets no cookies and runs no advertising or third-party tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its pages load their typeface from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, as any web host does, under GitHub's Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy. The page a coach sees after buying a team plan asks our server for the plan's claim code; to stop that being abused, our server keeps a one-way hash of the requesting IP address, never the address itself, and deletes it within two days.

We count visits to this website ourselves, on our own server, to see which pages are read and what brings people here. When a page opens, it sends us the page's address (never anything after the "?", apart from the utm_ tags in an ad's link), the website you came from, the width of your screen and your browser's language. While you are on the page it notes how long you actively read it, how far you scrolled, which sections you saw, and whether you tapped a link such as Download on the App Store. Our server adds the country your connection comes from and your device type and browser (for example "iPhone, Safari"), worked out from what your browser sends. To tell one visitor from another for a single day, it makes a one-way hash of your IP address and browser details mixed with a random value that is replaced every day; the old value is deleted, so the hash can't be traced back to you or linked to another day. Your IP address itself is never stored, nothing is stored on your device, and none of this is shared with anyone or linked to a Gamut account. Visit records are deleted after two years. To leave yourself out, add ?notrack=1 to any page's address once; the site then remembers, in your browser alone, not to count you.

The question form on the homepage is optional. If you use it, we store the email address you enter, whether you said you are an athlete, a parent, a coach or something else, your question if you wrote one, and whether you asked for updates. We use it only to reply to you and, if you ticked the box, to send you the occasional email about Gamut, which you can stop at any time by replying to one or emailing us. It is never sold, never shared for advertising, and not linked to a Gamut account. To stop the form being abused, our server counts submissions against a one-way hash of your IP address, deleted within two days as above. Messages are deleted after two years, or sooner if you ask.

Where your data is stored on your device

Your training data is kept on your phone so the app works without a connection, which matters in a gym with no signal. Your login session is stored separately in the iOS Keychain rather than in ordinary app storage, because a session token is a credential rather than app data. It is readable after you first unlock your phone following a restart, so the widgets can refresh while the phone is locked. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the Apple Watch app and the rest-timer Live Activity read a small summary kept on your phone. Progress photos are kept only on your phone, in Gamut's own storage. Gamut also keeps a note of which calendar you chose and which events it added, so it can update or remove exactly those.

How long we keep it

Your training data is kept for as long as your account exists, because its entire value is being a long record. Delete your account and it is removed immediately and permanently. The summary your coaches see is deleted when you turn sharing off, leave or are removed from the team, or the team's program ends. Your group memberships last until you leave the group or delete your account; a report of a group is kept while the group exists, or until the reporter deletes their account. Push tokens not seen for 60 days are deleted. Hashed IP addresses are deleted within two days. Records of visits to this website are deleted after two years. Messages sent through this website's question form are deleted after two years, or sooner if you ask.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear Training Data (in Settings) wipes every workout, set and record while keeping your account, username, friends and profile picture. Irreversible.
  • Delete My Account (in Settings) permanently deletes your account, your email address, your profile picture, your username and friends, your group memberships, and every row of training data in one operation, and removes your progress photos and the calendar events Gamut added from that phone. A group you created stays for its other members, without your name attached. There is no recovery and no grace period. It does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. If you are a team's head coach, deleting your account also ends the team's program, and its players and assistant coaches are told.
  • Remove your profile picture, delete a progress photo, or edit your body measurements on the Profile tab, at any time.
  • Groups: leave any group at any time; delete or rename a group you created.
  • Calendar: turn off "Workouts in Your Calendar" in Settings to remove the events Gamut added.
  • Findable by Email is off unless you turn it on in Settings; turn it off again at any time.
  • Team sharing and visibility: in Settings → Team, turn off sharing with your coaches, hide yourself from teammates, or leave the team. Coaches turn their alerts on or off in their team settings.
  • Notifications: each kind can be turned on or off in Settings.
  • Disconnect Apple Health: in the iOS Health app under Sharing → Apps → Gamut, turn off the categories you no longer want Gamut to read. Readings already cached on your phone are deleted when you sign out, switch accounts or delete the app.
  • Revoke Contacts, Photos, Camera or Calendars access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Revoking Contacts stops future contact searches from being run by you; it does not undo a match that already happened. Revoking Calendars stops Gamut updating the events it added but does not remove them; turn the feature off first if you want them gone.
  • Access and correction: your profile is editable in the app. For a copy of your data in a portable format, email us.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR (EU/UK) or the CCPA (California), including access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined by the CCPA. To exercise any right, email support@gamutapp.com.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS. Database access is restricted per account by row-level security policies enforced by the database itself. Your login session is stored in the iOS Keychain. No credential in the app can read or modify another account's private data.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you and any regulator required by law.

Children

Gamut is for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, the app does not let you set up a profile with an age under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Because the app prescribes resistance training loads, users under 16 should train under qualified supervision, such as a coach's. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Gamut from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed there, under appropriate safeguards for transfers out of the EEA and UK.

Changes to this policy, and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Questions or requests: support@gamutapp.com
